Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what information Vantralabz collects through this website, why, and how it's handled. If anything is unclear, email us at hello@vantralabz.com.

What we collect

We collect personal information only when you actively give it to us, through these routes on the site:

  • Contact form — your name, email address, a subject you select, and your message.
  • Project intake / "Book a call" flow — your name, email, optional WhatsApp number and company, the services you're interested in, your situation and timeline, your current website (if any), and any notes you add.
  • Newsletter signup — your email address.
  • Scheduling — if you book a call, the details you enter (name, email, time slot) are handled by Calendly.
  • WhatsApp and email — if you message us there, we see whatever you send.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't collect personal information passively just from you browsing the site.

How it's used

Form submissions and messages are used to respond to your inquiry, scope a project and schedule a call. Newsletter signups are used to occasionally send updates about Vantralabz's work; you can ask to be removed at any time.

Who else sees it

We rely on a few third-party services to run this site:

  • Formspree processes form submissions and delivers them to our inbox.
  • Calendly handles call scheduling.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics gives us aggregate, anonymized traffic statistics. It uses no cookies and doesn't track individual visitors.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers.
  • WhatsApp, if you choose to contact us there, under its own policy.

We don't sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries and project-related correspondence for as long as needed to respond and, if we work together, to run the engagement and meet our own record-keeping needs. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@vantralabz.com and we'll take care of it.

Children

This site is for businesses and isn't directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top always reflects the latest version.
