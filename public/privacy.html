Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what information Vantralabz collects through this website, why, and how it's handled. If anything here is unclear, email us at hello@vantralabz.com.

What we collect

We collect information only when you actively give it to us, through two forms on this site:

  • Contact form — your name, email address, a subject you select, and the message you write.
  • Newsletter signup — your email address.

We don't use tracking cookies, and we don't collect any personal information passively just from you browsing the site.

How it's used

Contact form submissions are used to respond to your inquiry — nothing more. Newsletter signups are used to occasionally send you updates about Vantralabz's work; you can ask to be removed at any time by emailing us.

Who else sees it

Form submissions are processed through Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, which delivers them to our inbox. We also use Plausible Analytics to see aggregate, anonymized traffic on this site — it doesn't use cookies and doesn't collect anything that identifies you personally.

We don't sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone, ever.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@vantralabz.com and we'll take care of it.

Changes to this policy

As the site grows, this policy may be updated to reflect that. The date at the top will always reflect the latest version.
